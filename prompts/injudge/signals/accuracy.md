2. accuracy (@accuracy@) — judge the claim in the situation it asserts.
   a. Identify that situation: a historical source, the current checkout, or
      general behavior under stated conditions. A problem-and-fix account
      describes its source situation even without an explicit time qualifier.
   b. Find evidence addressing that situation. Current code or successful use
      alone does not verify a historical account; matching identifiers or
      symptoms does not establish the same source state. General claims can
      be tested wherever their stated conditions hold.
   c. Assign corroborated if that evidence confirms the claim, contradicted
      if it refutes the claim, or uncontradicted if neither is established,
      including when the evidence-to-source connection is unknown.
   In the note, name the asserted situation and the evidence that connects
   to it, or the missing connection. Assess current applicability separately
   under relevance.
