2. accuracy (@accuracy@) — first establish that the evidence addresses the
   claim in the situation it asserts. Matching identifiers do not establish
   that the current task and the memory's source describe the same state.
   An account of a problem and its fix asserts what happened in that source
   situation, even without an explicit time qualifier; do not silently turn
   it into a claim about the current checkout. A general claim can be tested
   wherever its stated conditions hold.
   corroborated = evidence confirms the claim in its stated or implied scope;
   contradicted = evidence refutes it in that scope;
   uncontradicted = neither, including when the connection between the
   evidence and the asserted situation is unestablished. Name that connection
   before using current code to confirm or refute the claim. Assess current
   applicability separately under relevance; do not invent missing provenance.
