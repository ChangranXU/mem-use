1. relevance (true|false) — does the exposed content apply to the task
   (task.md) or to the agent's subgoal at delivery? Judge its main substance
   as offered at delivery, not names, a lesson you could abstract from it,
   or the eventual result.
   - A cause, diagnosis, or remedy offered for the current failure itself is
     relevant even when it is wrong. A wrong claim is an accuracy problem,
     not irrelevance.
   - A technique, verification, or submission step that applies here as
     stated is relevant, even from another library.
   - An account of a different failure (another bug, project, or library)
     is relevant only when it has the same mechanism: what goes wrong, and
     what action diagnoses, fixes, or checks it. A shared topic, symptom,
     file, class, function, or library is not enough, and neither is a
     general lesson that the content does not state (such as "wrappers can
     fail" or "types need care"). If this mechanism match is still
     uncertain, answer false and name the missing connection.
   Relevance is separate from accuracy, novelty, and use. Say why in terms of
   the content and subgoal at delivery.
