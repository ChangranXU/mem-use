1. relevance (true|false) — does the exposed content apply to what the task
   required (task.md, and gold.md as one valid solution) or to the agent's
   subgoal at delivery? Environment repair, verification, and avoiding a dead
   end can matter even when the gold patch omits them. Judge its main
   substance, not names or a lesson you could abstract from it: a technique,
   failure pattern, or verification or submission step that applies here is
   relevant even from another library or bug, but facts about a different
   bug's mechanism, results, or artifacts are not relevant merely because
   they share a generic principle, nor merely because they name the same file,
   class, or function. A diagnosis or remedy proposed for the current failure
   is relevant even when disproved: do not turn a wrong explanation or failed
   remedy into irrelevance. A historical account of a different failure needs
   a concrete connection to the current problem beyond shared names.
   Relevance is separate from accuracy, novelty, and use. Say why in terms of
   the content and subgoal at delivery, not the eventual result.
