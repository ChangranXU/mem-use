- gold.md shows one valid solution to the current task. Use it to assess
  relevance and, when it addresses the claim's scope, accuracy. Other
  solutions can be valid. A matching patch does not establish memory
  influence or benefit. Influence needs evidence from recorded actions and
  prior inputs. A benefit needs a recorded check or later use of the
  memory-shaped output or property; gold and task outcome alone are
  insufficient. Verification, environment repair, or an avoided dead end can
  help even when the gold patch omits them.
- A failing outcome does not identify the failing assertion. Differences
  from the gold patch alone cannot establish why the task failed; name a
  recorded diagnostic or leave the failure cause undetermined.
