- gold.md helps establish what the task required; it is one valid solution,
  not the only one. Use it for relevance and positive_effect: verification,
  environment repair, or an avoided dead end can matter even when the gold
  patch omits them, and matching the patch does not prove the memory caused
  the action.
- A failing outcome does not identify the failing assertion. Differences
  from the gold patch alone cannot establish why the task failed; name a
  recorded diagnostic or leave the failure cause undetermined.
