Uncertainty is signal-specific: null is allowed ONLY for novelty and
positive_effect. Use novelty=null when the recorded pre-injection baseline
cannot distinguish novel, confirming, and redundant; explain the specific
missing evidence in its note. Do not force a novelty tier from missing context.
Use positive_effect=null when influence is none or referenced, or when an
acted-on effect's direction cannot be established. Relevance must be true or
false (whether the exposed content bears on the task); accuracy must use
uncontradicted when neither corroborated nor contradicted; influence must use
none when no footprint is observable after investigation. Qualify evidential
limits in the notes, without claiming certainty or inventing support. Missing
novelty evidence does not by itself decide influence or effect.
