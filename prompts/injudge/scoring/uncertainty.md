the missing evidence) and positive_effect (influence none or referenced, or
the direction cannot be established). Relevance is true or false; accuracy is
uncontradicted when neither corroborated nor contradicted; influence is none
when no footprint is found, and its note says influence was not established,
not that the memory had no effect. Qualify limits in notes; never invent
support. Missing novelty evidence does not decide influence or effect.
